Construction and clear space

Three solid modules hold the base and the fourth turns into a leaf that steps out of the grid. Always use the master artwork; never redraw it.

Module 50 · gutter 6 · corner radius 9 · leaf radius 40, offset 6 up and right
x
Clear space: x = the height of one symbol module, on every side
Minimum sizes

Horizontal logo: 30 mm wide in print, 120 px on screen.

Symbol: 8 mm in print, 24 px on screen (16 px for favicons only).

Below these sizes the leaf and the “o” lose definition. Use the symbol alone instead of shrinking the logo.

Colour balance and contrast

Most of a page is Lab Ink, white and Mist. Green is used in small, deliberate amounts. Check text against these pairings before you publish.

Balance
Lab Ink 40%White 35%Mist 10%Greens 15%

Rules of thumb

  • Use Lab Ink text on Grow Green, never white, for anything smaller than a headline.
  • On light backgrounds, green text is Deep Green. On Lab Ink, it is Mint.
  • One green highlight per area: a button, a number or a leaf, not all three.
  • No gradients in the logo or the palette. Flat colour only.

Bangla and bilingual type

Many of our customers read Bangla first. Bangla text uses Hind Siliguri, which sits well next to Inter and Space Grotesk.

Bangla
অআ কখ

Hind Siliguri

Regular 400 for text, SemiBold 600 for headings, Bold 700 sparingly.

০১২৩৪৫৬৭৮৯ · গ্রোটেক ল্যাব

নিরাপদ অনলাইন, নিশ্চিন্ত জীবন।
Staying safe online, in plain Bangla and English.

Rules

  • Set Bangla at about 110% of the English size next to it; Bangla letters look smaller at the same point size.
  • Give Bangla more line height: 1.5 to 1.7 for text.
  • Keep each language in its own line or block. Don’t switch scripts inside a sentence, except for names.
  • Use Bangla numerals in Bangla text and Latin numerals in English text. Phone numbers and web addresses stay in Latin.
  • Never use a fake bold or a stretched font. Use the real SemiBold and Bold files.

Graphic elements

A few simple pieces, all taken from the symbol, make layouts feel like growtech without repeating the logo everywhere.

Module pattern

The symbol’s rounded modules, enlarged and set in Ink 2 (#1A2436) on Lab Ink, with one Grow Green leaf. Use it on covers, banners and the last page, cropped off the edge. One pattern per page, always behind the content.

Line icons

2 px rounded strokes in Mint, on a Lab Ink tile with a 3 mm corner.

Labels and chips
POS and barcodeInventoryBangla UIFlutterIn development

Pill shapes in Mist or Ink 2. Short words only.

Corners

Round corners at about 18% of the shorter side, like the symbol’s modules. Never sharp corners on cards and photos.

Photography

Real people at work: our team, trainees in class, local shop owners using the software. Natural light, honest settings. Avoid generic stock photos of handshakes and glowing screens; replace stand-in photos with our own as soon as we have them.
