GrowTech Labs is a software and digital firm from Bangladesh. We design, build and look after the systems a business depends on: sales and stock for shops, ERP and CRM for distributors, websites and apps for brands, and the automation that ties them together.

Our team has delivered work for a plastic-surgery practice in New York, a car-rental and car-wash business in the Netherlands, a multi-branch restaurant chain in Holland, and restaurants, bakeries, manufacturers and retailers across Dhaka.
